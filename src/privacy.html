PRIVACY BOUNDARY

Local unless you choose otherwise.

Pacefold has no account system, analytics, advertising or cloud database. Nothing below is proxied or kept by Pacefold: your browser talks to each service directly, and only when that feature is in use.

LOCAL

Stored here

Preferences, notes, the day log, cue state and timers live in this browser's storage. They leave only in a backup you download or a live backup file you pick.

OPTIONAL

Weather

With weather on, your saved coordinates go to Open-Meteo for the forecast and air quality. Location names are never shown on Clock, the start page or the weather sheet.

ON REQUEST

Radar

Opening the radar fetches frames for the area around you: Environment and Climate Change Canada in Canada, RainViewer elsewhere, on an unlabelled CARTO / OpenStreetMap base map.

OPTIONAL

Music and OneNote

Music plays through YouTube's official player only when you load it. With Microsoft access set up, Pacefold copies unsynced notes to the OneNote section you choose (delegated Notes.ReadWrite).
