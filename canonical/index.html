Pacefold now has one public surface and one current release gate. The proven local engine remains underneath for continuity, while obsolete visual layers and historical product concepts no longer define the experience.

Quiet actions

Log the small things in one tap.

Hydration, preparation timer, rest, meals, eye resets and movement use explicit states and leave an honest local record.

Daybook

The notebook stays in the room.

A visible lower fold shows today, recent notes and quick paths to Notes, Worklog, Now and Settings—without modal clutter.

Personal rhythm

Faith-aware when useful. Neutral when not.

Use location-aware Islamic prayer times, everyday reset moments, mindfulness, another editable preset or a completely custom schedule.

Private by design

Your workday is not an analytics product.

No account, advertising or behavioural tracking. Notes and logs stay in the browser, with a backup you can download and keep.

Installable

Feels like a small desktop instrument.

Install Pacefold from Edge or Chrome for a focused window, offline shell and taskbar cues. It still works as a normal website when installation is unavailable.

Four principles

Calm is a behaviour, not decoration.

The interface is designed around fewer owners, fewer hidden states and fewer reasons to reopen settings. It remembers what you chose and keeps the current action obvious.
